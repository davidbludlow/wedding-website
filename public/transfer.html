Privacy & technical details

Data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and sent directly between browsers over a WebRTC peer-to-peer connection. The encryption key is embedded in the link's #fragment, which browsers never send to any server. As of 2026, AES-256 is believed to provide strong resistance against both classical and quantum computing attacks.

A Google STUN server (stun.l.google.com) is used solely to help each browser discover its own public IP address so a direct connection can be established. The STUN server never sees, relays, or has access to your data or encryption key. Google may observe the IP addresses and timing of the connection request, but nothing else.

No data is stored anywhere. Once both parties close the page, nothing remains.
