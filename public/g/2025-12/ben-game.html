Welcome to Robot Holiday Rumble 🎄

Two-player local game: both players wrap opponents into presents to immobilize them. Wrapping paper items respawn in the maze—each gives one charge and must be re-collected after every attempt (successful or not). Stay in close range and hold your attack key to apply a hit; 3 hits defeats the opponent.

Controls & Tips

  • Player 1 (Wrapper): W A S D to move, Left Shift to attack (needs wrapping paper)
  • Player 2 (Wrapper): Arrows to move, L to attack (needs wrapping paper)
  • You must be within close range (visible on-screen) and hold attack for ~1 second to score one successful hit. Repeat 3 times to defeat.
  • Pick up wrapping paper from the maze — each item gives exactly one wrapping charge.
  • Walls block movement—use corridors to flank the opponent.
