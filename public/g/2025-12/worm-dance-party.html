Wiggle Party

A single-file wormy party arcade game (original)

How to play

You’re the glow-worm. Eat snacks to grow and score points. Avoid rocks, mushrooms, and the other party worms.

Fill the Party Meter by eating confetti stars. When full: PARTY TIME starts—score multiplier, invincible, and extra sparkles.

Chill: fewer enemies, slower pace.
Party: balanced, classic.
Chaos: more worms + faster spawns.
